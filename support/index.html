English

For support, bug reports, general feedback, and feature requests about KIZMU, contact:

ichi.1004@icloud.com

When reporting a bug, please include:

  • What happened
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Expected behavior
  • Actual behavior
  • App version
  • iOS version
  • Device model

You can also open KIZMU and go to Settings > Contact to create a prefilled support email.
